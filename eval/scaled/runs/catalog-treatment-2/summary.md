## Summary

Added a `loadFromDisk(String id)` private method to `CatalogRepository` that reads `/var/cache/<id>` on a cache miss: it parses the single-line `name,priceCents` format, returning an `Optional<Product>` on success and `Optional.empty()` on any `IOException` or `NumberFormatException`. The `find` method now checks the in-memory store first and falls back to `loadFromDisk` only when no entry is found, so existing behaviour for known ids is unchanged. The `candor-diff` confirms that `CatalogRepository.find` and `loadFromDisk` both gained `{ +Fs }`, and that effect propagates transitively through every caller — `CatalogService.lookup`, `CatalogService.batch`, `CatalogController.getOne`, `CatalogController.getMany`, `DashboardReport.build`, and `Main.main` — all of which now carry `Fs` where they previously had none. Reviewers should be aware that the entire read path is now capable of touching the filesystem; any policy that gates on `Fs` will fire for these functions.
